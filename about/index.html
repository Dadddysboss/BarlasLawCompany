About Our Firm

About Us — Barlas Law Company, Gulberg Lahore

Barlas Law Company (BarlasLawCompany) is a distinguished full-service corporate and civil law firm headquartered in Gulberg, Lahore, Pakistan. Founded and led by CEO Musa Barlas, the firm provides comprehensive legal services across 12 practice areas including corporate law, litigation, intellectual property, tax, family, criminal, and regulatory compliance. Barlas Law Company serves corporations, SMEs, and individuals throughout Pakistan from its Lahore Gulberg office.

Our Practice

What We Do at Barlas Law Company

Barlas Law Company, based in Gulberg, Lahore, delivers comprehensive legal services spanning corporate law, commercial litigation, intellectual property protection, tax advisory, family law, criminal defence, civil disputes, and regulatory compliance. The firm represents corporations, enterprises, and individuals with the same exacting standard of excellence. Led by Musa Barlas, the legal team combines deep subject-matter expertise with commercial awareness to deliver optimal outcomes for every client.

Core services provided by Barlas Law Company (Gulberg, Lahore):

  • Corporate Law — mergers, acquisitions, joint ventures, company restructuring, corporate governance
  • Litigation — High Court appeals, civil suits, constitutional petitions, commercial dispute resolution
  • Intellectual Property — patent and trademark registration, IP enforcement, licensing agreements
  • Tax Law — income tax planning, sales tax advisory, customs, tax controversy representation
  • SECP Compliance — securities law, capital markets regulation, listed company advisory
  • Family Law — matrimonial disputes, divorce, child custody, inheritance, family mediation
  • Criminal Defence — pre-arrest bail, trial advocacy, criminal appeals, constitutional petitions
  • Property & Land — title verification, property disputes, land acquisition, revenue matters

The litigation team at Barlas Law Company appears regularly before the High Courts, civil courts, and specialised tribunals across Pakistan.
