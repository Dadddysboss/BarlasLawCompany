Practice Areas

Our Services

From corporate restructuring to family disputes, our firm provides skilled representation across every major area of Pakistani law.

Corporate Law

Comprehensive corporate counsel including company incorporation, mergers and acquisitions, joint ventures, corporate governance, and regulatory compliance for businesses of all sizes.

Intellectual Property

Protection and enforcement of patents, trademarks, copyrights, and trade secrets. We handle IP registration, licensing, and litigation to safeguard your innovations and brand value.

Competition

Antitrust and competition law advisory, merger control filings, abuse of dominance assessments, and competition compliance programmes tailored to Pakistani and regional regulatory frameworks.

Arbitration / Mediation

Alternative dispute resolution services including domestic and international arbitration, mediation, and conciliation. We help clients resolve commercial disputes efficiently and cost-effectively.

Tax

Expert tax planning and controversy services covering income tax, sales tax, customs duties, and international tax matters. We represent clients before tax authorities and appellate tribunals.

Family

Compassionate representation in matrimonial disputes, child custody, divorce, inheritance, and family mediation. We prioritise amicable resolutions while protecting your legal rights.

Criminal

Robust criminal defence representation from pre-arrest bail through trial and appeal. We protect the rights of the accused at every stage of the criminal justice process.

Civil

Broad civil litigation practice covering contract disputes, tort claims, property disputes, debt recovery, and injunctions. We appear before civil courts throughout Pakistan.

Securities SECP & Financial Law

Regulatory compliance and representation before the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP). Advisory on capital markets, listed companies, and financial services regulation.

Immigration

Immigration services including work visas, investor visas, family sponsorship, and residency applications. We guide clients through Pakistan's immigration framework with precision and care.

Land / Property / Revenue

Expert advisory on land acquisition, property disputes, title verification, revenue matters, and real estate transactions. We navigate Pakistan's complex land laws with confidence.

Private International Law

Cross-border legal advisory covering conflict of laws, international contracts, cross-jurisdictional disputes, and enforcement of foreign judgments and arbitral awards.
